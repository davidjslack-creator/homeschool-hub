How a week runs

  1. David approves the weekly plan
    Nothing runs until he says yes. He is teacher of record and supervises all use.
  2. Daily lessons from Miacademy
    7 active Gradebook courses set the scope and order. Language Arts and Math run Mon–Fri, with Reading Comprehension on Friday; Structures of Science Mon–Fri; U.S. Geography Tue/Wed/Thu; Typing Mon–Thu.
  3. Tue and Thu: Tutor Time with Christen Sinyard
    She leads; the Coordinator briefs her before and maps her work after.
  4. Wed 1:00–1:40 PM: Singing with Kathleen Blanco
    A fixed part of the week.
  5. Friday: progress report
    Daily logs and Friday reports build the portfolio for Kenny's annual evaluation, next due Oct 31, 2027.
